Why Reverse-Cycle AC Makes Sense

The numbers make it a no-brainer

🌡️

One System. Every Season.

Modern reverse-cycle inverter systems heat your home in winter and cool it in summer — one installation that works hard all year. No separate heater, no separate AC unit, no separate running costs.

⚡

Way Cheaper to Heat Than Gas or Bar Heaters

Reverse-cycle inverter systems produce 3–5 units of heat for every 1 unit of electricity used. That makes them 3–5x more efficient than electric bar heaters and significantly cheaper to run than most gas systems.

FAQ

Your questions, answered clearly

How much does AC installation cost in Australia in 2026?
A standard 2.5–3.5kW reverse-cycle split system installation typically costs $1,200–$1,800 fully installed. A larger 6kW system runs $1,800–$2,800. Multi-head systems for 2–4 rooms range from $4,000–$8,000. Ducted reverse-cycle systems for a whole home start around $8,000 and can reach $20,000+ depending on home size and existing ducting. Use our free matching service for a personalised fixed-price quote.
What size air conditioner do I need for my home?
As a rough guide: 2.5kW for a small bedroom (~15m²), 3.5kW for a standard bedroom or study (~25m²), 5–6kW for a large bedroom or open-plan living (~40m²), 7–9kW for large open-plan areas (~60m²+). Homes in hotter or more humid climates often need slightly more capacity. Your matched installer will calculate the exact size based on your room dimensions, ceiling height, insulation, and sun exposure.
Split system or ducted — which is right for my home?
Split systems (wall-mounted indoor unit + outdoor compressor) are ideal for 1–3 rooms, offering excellent efficiency and value. Ducted systems cool your entire home from a single outdoor unit via ceiling vents — seamless whole-home comfort with no visible indoor units. Multi-head systems bridge the two, running 2–4 indoor units from one outdoor unit. Your matched installer will recommend the best option for your layout, budget, and lifestyle.
What is ARCtick certification?
ARCtick (Australian Refrigeration Council) certification means your installer is legally authorised to handle refrigerants — a legal requirement for all refrigeration and AC work in Australia. Using an uncertified installer is illegal and may void your manufacturer warranty. Every installer in the AirconNow Technicians network holds current ARCtick certification and is fully insured.

Every installer in our network must hold current ARCtick certification, carry appropriate public liability and worker's compensation insurance, maintain verifiable 4★+ customer reviews, and meet our workmanship quality standards. How long does AC installation take?
A standard single split system installation typically takes 2–4 hours. Multi-head installations may take a full day. New ducted systems with significant roof work can take 1–3 days depending on home size. Your matched installer will provide a clear timeline when delivering your quote.
